Everything about plans, payments and your gold balance, in plain words.

How does TDF Gold work?

You choose a savings plan and pay a monthly installment. Each payment is converted to gold at the live rate, and your balance and receipts are always visible in the app.

How long does a payment take to reflect?

UPI and card payments are usually credited within minutes. In rare cases, bank processing can take one to two business days.

What if I pay more than my installment?

On the Gold Saving Scheme, any surplus is credited as additional gold on that installment. It does not roll into next month's installment.

Why does Bandhan show "Amount Invested"?

Bandhan locks its gold rate at the final installment, so until then the app shows what you have invested rather than grams held.

Can I close my plan early?

Foreclosure rules depend on your plan. Check the plan details in the app or contact support for your specific scheme.
